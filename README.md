# 🎮 Pixel Quest: 30-Day Challenge

A cute, anime-inspired pixel art habit tracker designed to help you conquer your goals over 30 days. Level up your life by tracking daily wins in workouts, clean eating, learning, and coding!

![Pixel Quest Aesthetic](https://img.shields.io/badge/Aesthetic-Kawaii-ff99c8)
![Tech](https://img.shields.io/badge/Tech-React--Tailwind-blue)

## ✨ Features

- **30-Day Progress Map**: All 30 days are available from the start. Complete all 4 habits to earn a gold star for the day.
…- **Lavender Purple**: `#D0A9F5` (Soft shadows)
- **Mint Blue**: `#A0E7E5` (UI accents)
- **Text Color**: `#4A4E69` (High contrast retro text)
